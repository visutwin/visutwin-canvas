Sparse Grass Textures
=====================

The ground textures of the crowd example - a scanned material from Poly Haven, released into the
public domain (CC0 1.0 Universal, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Each is the
1k JPG of the diffuse color and of the OpenGL normal map, unchanged.

sparse_grass_diff_1k.jpg, sparse_grass_nor_1k.jpg
    "Sparse Grass" by Amal Kumar - https://polyhaven.com/a/sparse_grass
