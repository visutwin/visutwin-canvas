Modifications:
The published scene was downloaded as an uncompressed 3DGS PLY (1,680,237 splats,
154 MB) and converted to the SuperSplat compressed PLY format with
tools/ply_to_compressed_ply.py: splats below 0.02 alpha were dropped, the cloud
was thinned to 500,000 splats, and spherical harmonics were discarded. Geometry
and colour are otherwise unchanged.
